# VISUAL REFERENCES - Coreline Defense

This file records approved visual references and how to translate them into this project without copying their IP, layout, or exact assets.

## Approved reference direction - 2026-06-23

The owner provided a dark pixel-art main-menu reference and a dungeon-like gameplay GIF. The binary reference files are not committed; this document keeps the approved direction as text.

## What to learn from them

- Dark pixel-art mood with deep navy, blue-grey, muted purple shadows.
- Large readable pixel clusters, low noise, clear silhouettes.
- Background depth through large shapes: distant hills/walls/fortress-like structures, dark foreground framing.
- Small warm amber lights as focal points, like torches in the reference.
- Gameplay floor has readable material variation: slabs, cracks, debris patches, border walls, and gate elements.
- Menu has a central hero pixel scene, dark frame, large title, and compact vertical menu.

## Translation for Coreline Defense

- Castle / dungeon walls -> sci-fi outpost walls, bunker plating, defense rail, alien breach gate.
- Torch flames -> amber warning beacons, reactor lamps, signal flares, energy lanterns.
- Stone floor -> industrial stone-metal slabs, cracked plating, cable/debris patches.
- Fantasy hero silhouette -> small armored commander/scout silhouette for background storytelling only.
- Moon / stars mood -> distant planet, orbital debris, radar pings, signal stars.

## Rules

- Do not copy the reference logo, exact composition, characters, or medieval props.
- Keep Coreline Defense sci-fi: alien robots, defense grid, reactor energy, rail cannon, tactical campaign route.
- Use warm amber lights sparingly against cold blue/purple backgrounds.
- Gameplay readability wins over decoration: lanes/cells, units, enemies, projectiles, and orbs must remain clear on mobile.
